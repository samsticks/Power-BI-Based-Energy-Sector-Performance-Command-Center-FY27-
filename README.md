#Project Background and Key Value Delivered

Designed and developed an enterprise-grade Power BI Executive Command Center for Energy Sector leadership to monitor wins, pipeline performance, service line contributions, client insights, forecast revenue, and target achievement across Nigeria and Ghana.

The solution automated the ingestion and consolidation of weekly Energy Sector reports stored in SharePoint, enabling real-time performance monitoring without manual report rebuilding as new weekly files became available.

This analytics solution served as a strategic decision-support platform by enabling:

• Executive Performance Monitoring
Provided leadership with a consolidated view of Total Wins, Pipeline Value, Forecast Revenue, Revenue Achievement, Pipeline Achievement, and Win Rate across multiple service lines and geographies.

• Automated Weekly Reporting
Implemented a SharePoint Folder + Power Query architecture that automatically ingests and appends new weekly reports (WK1, WK2, WK3 … WK52) without requiring report redesign or manual intervention.

• Service Line Performance Tracking
Delivered dynamic visibility into Consulting, Assurance, Tax, and SaT performance, helping leadership monitor contribution, target achievement, revenue gaps, and forecast attainment.

• Pipeline Intelligence & Forecasting
Developed probability-adjusted forecasting models, pipeline coverage analysis, stage distribution tracking, and near-term revenue prediction to improve revenue planning and pipeline management.

• Client & Sector Analytics
Created client-level and sector-level insight dashboards enabling stakeholders to identify key revenue-generating accounts, sector concentration, geographic impact, and service line penetration across the Energy portfolio.

• Executive Decision Support
Provided highly interactive dashboards with drill-down analysis, week-over-week trend tracking, fiscal quarter reporting, and dynamic slicers that support strategic decision-making and portfolio optimization.

• Advanced Data Modelling
Built a scalable star-schema data model incorporating:

Fact_Wins
Fact_Pipe
Dim_Week
Fiscal Date Dimension
Service Line Target Framework
ensuring high-performance reporting and analytical flexibility.
